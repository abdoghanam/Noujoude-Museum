--- WHAT THE RE-RUN CONFIRMS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT -----------------------------------------------
Confirms: the route table is served by a real process; the root redirect is temporary and targets /en;
direction follows the locale segment; malformed, unknown and encoded slugs are refused with 404 rather
than rendered; nothing indexable is exposed; no content the registry did not supply appears.
Does not confirm: accessibility (no audit run), performance (no measurement), authentication or the
Control Center (not built), any published heritage fact (none exists), rights to any image (0 of 82),
or reproducibility on a second environment (this is a host-class run on one machine, which is exactly
the ceiling §16 of SECURITY_AND_PERMISSIONS.md and CAP-10 place on the credit it can earn).
Open consequence of the corrected line above: `next start` binds the wildcard address with no option
set, so a local serve is reachable on the machine's other interfaces. That is not a production exposure
(nothing is deployed and nothing is published), but it is a decision this project has never made
explicitly, and it is registered for the Owner rather than silently hardened here.
