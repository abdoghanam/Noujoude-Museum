SCOPE

The master specification defines the product, public experience, information
architecture, pages, editorial model, collection and artifact data model,
curatorial evidence, localization, publication states, visual system, media
and rights governance, AI Curator, the 20-module Master Control Center, roles
and permissions, publishing operations, privacy, security, quality standards,
acceptance criteria, Owner inputs, and canonical conflict resolutions.

SOURCE TRACEABILITY NOTE

The CSV preserves the exact original text of each logical source line and maps
all lines to contiguous source ranges and canonical master destinations. The
range-level disposition is a traceability locator; it is not a claim that every
individual line received a separate independent semantic verdict. Repeated
wording was consolidated, unique requirements were retained in canonical
sections, and material that is only an implementation-state claim was moved
to an implementation-verification process rather than treated as product law.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

- This is a product and system-behavior specification, not proof of implementation.
- The logical data model is not a verified physical database schema.
- The 72 original asset filenames and 10 additional requested filenames are
  reference requirements, not proof that files exist, are generated, cleared,
  integrated, or production-ready.
- Institutional facts, actual collection inventory, ownership/provenance evidence,
  image/document rights, legal identity, jurisdiction, and final Owner approvals
  remain controlled inputs where the source does not establish them.
- Standards cited in the document are targets/reference bases, not a claim of
  current WCAG, Core Web Vitals, or OWASP conformance by the live website.

HANDOFF RULE

Once formally approved by the authorized Owner, this document is the single
normative product baseline. Design files, technical architecture, implementation
plans, test cases, and operational procedures may elaborate it but must not
silently contradict it. Changes require a recorded change request and approval.
